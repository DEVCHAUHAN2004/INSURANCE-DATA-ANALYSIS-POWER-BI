## 📊 Project Overview

**Secure Shield Insurance Pvt. Ltd.** is an interactive **Insurance Data Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard transforms insurance data into meaningful business insights by analyzing:

* 💰 Premium Amount
* 🛡️ Coverage Amount
* 💸 Claim Amount
* 📋 Policy Status
* 🚗 Policy Types
* 👥 Customer Demographics
* 📈 Claim Trends
* 🔄 Active vs Inactive Policies

This project demonstrates practical knowledge of **Data Cleaning, Power Query, Data Modeling, DAX, Data Visualization and Business Intelligence**.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objective of this project is to help an insurance company understand its **policy, premium and claim performance**.

### Key Questions Answered

* What is the total premium generated?
* What is the total coverage amount?
* What is the total claim amount?
* Which policy type generates the highest premium?
* How many policies are active and inactive?
* What is the distribution of claims by status?
* How do claim amounts vary across age groups?
* How are claims distributed across different policy types?

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 💰 Premium Amount by Policy Type

Premium amounts are analyzed across:

* ✈️ Travel
* 🏥 Health
* 🚗 Auto
* ❤️ Life
* 🏠 Home

**Travel Insurance** contributes the highest premium amount among the policy categories.

### 🔄 Active vs Inactive Policies

The dashboard provides a quick overview of policy activity:

* 🟢 **Active Policies:** 7.49K
* 🔵 **Inactive Policies:** 2.51K

### 📋 Number of Claims by Claim Status

Claims are categorized into:

* ❌ Rejected
* ✅ Settled
* ⏳ Pending

### 👥 Claim Amount by Age Group

Claim amounts are analyzed across:

* 🧑 Young Adult
* 👨 Adult
* 👴 Elder

### 📊 Claim Analysis by Policy Type

A detailed matrix compares **Pending, Rejected and Settled** claims across:

* Auto
* Health
* Home
* Life
* Travel
